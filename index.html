Abdullah Al Mahfuz Fahim

Researcher & Student of Al-Hadith and Islamic Studies

Abdullah Al Mahfuz Fahim is a student of Al-Hadith and Islamic Studies at Islamic University, Kushtia, and a researcher associated with the Ali Shariati Study Center and Dhaka International Research Institute. His academic interests encompass Islamic theology, philosophy, Hadith and Qur'anic studies, critical and analytical thinking, cinema, art and performing arts, and contemporary questions concerning Muslim society and civilization. He is also the Founder and Chairman of Nuha Institute, an intellectual initiative dedicated to fostering research, knowledge, critical inquiry, and contemporary Islamic thought. His research interests increasingly explore the intersection of Islamic knowledge, education, philosophy, and emerging technologies, including Artificial Intelligence.
